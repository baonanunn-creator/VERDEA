Concept & Design

The Story Behind
a Verdant Oasis

Discover the design philosophy behind VERDÉA HOTEL - where nature and architecture engage in dialogue.

Concept

A Sanctuary Within Nature

Amid the fast pace of Saigon, VERDÉA HOTEL was envisioned as a quiet green retreat - where the boundary between architecture and nature gradually softens. Every design decision serves a single purpose: bringing nature closer to urban life.

The hotel is more than a place to stay; it is an emotional journey - one where every guest is invited to slow down, observe, and reconnect with themselves through spaces alive with natural materiality.

The name VERDÉA is drawn from “verde” - the green of life itself - a quiet promise of a place that always feels fresh, always breathing with nature.

Every element at VERDÉA is developed around five core design principles.

01 - Rooted in Nature

Dissolving the Indoor-Outdoor Boundary

02 - Natural Materials

04 - Organic Form

Soft Lines, Free of Symmetry

05 - Multi-Sensory Design

Awakening Every Sense

The Emotional Journey

Slow Down · Connect · Recharge

Three stages shape the VERDÉA stay, from the moment guests step into the lobby to the moment they depart.

Stage 01

Slow Down

The rhythm of the city gently fades as guests step through the entrance, welcomed by greenery and the soft light of the main lobby.

Stage 02

Connect

Shared spaces are designed to invite conversation and quiet moments alike, with nature and with fellow guests.

Stage 03

Recharge

Every service, from dining to wellness, is oriented toward restoring body and mind for the journey ahead.

Location

In the Heart of Thao Dien, Ho Chi Minh City

VERDÉA HOTEL is located at 215A Nguyen Van Huong Street, Thao Dien Ward - a short, convenient ride from the city centre, and minutes from key attractions, shopping and the financial district.
