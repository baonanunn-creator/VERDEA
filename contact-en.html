A Few Things Worth Knowing

Cancellation Policy

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. A one-night charge applies for cancellations made after this window.

Pets

VERDÉA does not currently accommodate pets, with the exception of trained service animals assisting guests with disabilities.

Airport Transfers

Transfer service to and from Tan Son Nhat Airport is available on request; please contact us at least 24 hours in advance.
