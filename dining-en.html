Dining

Botanical Dining

Three dining destinations - One philosophy: Honouring local ingredients amid lush surroundings.

Main Restaurant

All Day Dining

An open space wrapped in greenery, serving Vietnamese and international cuisine throughout the day. The menu changes with the seasons, prioritising fresh ingredients sourced from local farms.

A generous breakfast buffet, a light lunch set menu and an extensive dinner selection for families and discerning diners alike.

  • · Hours: 6:00 AM - 10:30 PM
  • · Seating for 120 guests
  • · Garden-facing outdoor terrace
Signature Restaurant

Specialty Restaurant

A destination for intimate, refined evenings, where the tasting menu is built around the story of each ingredient - from the highlands of the Northwest to the Mekong Delta.

An intimate space with warm, golden light and views onto the internal garden, ideal for anniversary dinners or hosting important partners.

  • · Hours: 6:00 PM - 10:30 PM
  • · Reservations recommended
  • · Seasonal menu & tasting menu
Sky Bar

Rooftop Bar & Lounge

Set on the hotel's highest floor, the Sky Bar offers panoramic views of the city skyline glittering by night. The cocktail list draws on the herbs and tropical fruits of Vietnam.

The perfect spot to close out a long day with a favourite cocktail and a cool breeze from above.

  • · Hours: 5:00 PM - Midnight
  • · Live music on weekends
  • · Outdoor high-table seating
A Space to Unwind

Botanical Lounge

A waiting and lounge area wrapped in a man-made waterfall and living green walls - ideal for an afternoon tea or an easy, unhurried conversation.
