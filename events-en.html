Events & MICE

Spaces For
Life's Important Moments

From corporate conferences to intimate weddings, VERDÉA offers flexible spaces amid lush surroundings.

Grand Hall

Verdéa Grand Hall

The hotel's principal venue for large-scale events: conferences, product launches, weddings. Soaring ceilings paired with a modern sound and lighting system, accented by VERDÉA's signature hanging greenery.

  • · Capacity: up to 300 guests (banquet style)
  • · Full sound, lighting & LED display system
  • · Private entrance & pre-function reception area
Meeting Room

Meetings & Conferences

A flexible space suited to small and mid-sized meetings, internal training or partner gatherings. Natural light and garden views help sustain focus and inspiration throughout the day.

  • · Capacity: 20-80 guests, layout dependent
  • · Full presentation equipment
  • · Tea break & catering service included
Boardroom

Private Boardroom

A discreet, refined space for high-level meetings, important negotiations or private interviews. Warm timber interiors are paired with modern video-conferencing technology.

  • · Capacity: up to 16 guests
  • · Video-conferencing system
  • · Dedicated secretarial & technical support
