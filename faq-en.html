Location & Concept

VERDÉA HOTEL is located at 215A Nguyen Van Huong Street, Thao Dien Ward, Ho Chi Minh City. This location was carefully selected because it sits in a tranquil, lush area that perfectly aligns with our design philosophy.

VERDÉA HOTEL stands out through its open spaces filled with greenery and natural materials, maximizing natural light and airflow to create a peaceful sanctuary in the heart of the bustling city. We don't just provide accommodation—we deliver a holistic experience that helps you slow down, connect with nature, and rejuvenate your spirit.

This concept reflects our core philosophy: creating a refuge from the pressures of urban life, where nature is seamlessly integrated into every architectural and interior design detail. VERDÉA HOTEL is where you can breathe fresh air, hear the sound of flowing water and rustling leaves, and feel nature's peace—all while remaining within the vibrant heart of the city.

Our design maximizes natural light and ventilation through:

  • Expansive glass walls that allow linear sunlight to flood common areas
  • Open design that enables natural cross-ventilation throughout the hotel
  • Living green walls and water features that naturally regulate temperature
  • Result: Significantly reduced electricity consumption for lighting and cooling systems

Nature is the primary design language of VERDÉA HOTEL, reflected through five core principles:

  • Nature Integration: Integrated green canopies, living walls, and water features
  • Natural Materials: Prioritizing wood, stone, and sustainable materials
  • Light & Air: Maximizing open design and natural airflow
  • Organic Forms: Mimicking natural lines, reducing harsh concrete angles
  • Multisensory Design: Combining natural light, water sounds, and plant aromas

Sustainability

VERDÉA HOTEL implements sustainability through five core design principles:

  • Nature Integration: Integrated green canopies, living walls, and water features reduce urban heat effect and provide natural temperature regulation
  • Natural Materials: Prioritizing wood, stone, and sustainably sourced materials while minimizing synthetic, polluting materials
  • Light & Air: Maximizing open design and natural cross-ventilation to significantly reduce electricity consumption for lighting and cooling
  • Organic Forms: Mimicking natural lines, reducing rigid concrete angles to optimize airflow and energy distribution
  • Multisensory Design: Combining natural light, water sounds, and plant aromas to create a healthy living environment that enhances both physical and mental wellbeing

VERDÉA HOTEL is called an "urban sanctuary" because we have successfully created an environment that escapes the noise and pollution of modern city life. Every aspect of our design—from integrated greenery to natural materials, from open design enabling natural airflow to organic forms—is focused on delivering a sustainable, healthy, and peaceful living environment for every guest.
