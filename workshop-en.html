Workshops inspired by nature, the senses and local cuisine - where every experience becomes a form of Green Therapy at the heart of VERDÉA.

Nature Workshop

Terrarium Workshop – Building a Miniature Ecosystem

Design your own self-contained ecosystem inside a recycled glass vessel, layered with fresh moss, organic soil, mineral gravel and succulents. A Green Therapy experience that invites you to slow down and take home a piece of VERDÉA's “green oasis.”

  • · 60 - 90 minute session
  • · Recycled & organic materials
  • · Includes indoor plant-care guidance
Nature Workshop

Organic Floral Workshop – The Art of Local, Green Blooms

An Organic Floral Design experience using 100% seasonal, local flowers - with no plastic floral foam. Instead, a traditional Kenzan pin frog or woven rattan structure is used, honouring natural beauty and VERDÉA's commitment to sustainability.

  • · 100% seasonal, local organic blooms
  • · No plastic waste (No Floral Foam)
  • · Held in the garden, Yoga area or Lobby Lounge
Sensory & Local Crafts

VERDÉA Scent Lab – A Personal Fragrance Studio

Explore fragrance families - floral, herbal, woody and citrus - and freely blend them into a scent of your own, guided by our team. Your creation is bottled with VERDÉA's signature packaging, a keepsake tied to your stay.

  • · No perfumery experience required
  • · A multi-sensory experience centred on scent
  • · A personalised creation to take home
Organic F&B & Connection

Verdéa Tea Sanctuary – Herbal Tea & Mindfulness

Wander through the organic herb garden, hand-picking tea leaves and flowers, before blending a personalised detox tea recipe with expert guidance. The tasting unfolds alongside gentle breathing exercises and a short meditation to restore mental wellbeing.

  • · Garden tour & hands-on herb picking
  • · A personalised tea blend suited to you
  • · Paired with gentle breathing & meditation
